Where you'll live

Villa Kassar, El Baeirat

A private villa on Luxor's West Bank, among the sugarcane fields and close to the tombs. The whole house is ours for the week: no other guests, no hotel lobby. Classes happen here every morning, and dinner is usually on the roof.

Before you fly

Packing for Luxor in December

Days are warm and sunny, around 22–24°C. Nights and early mornings are cold, close to 6–8°C, and the balloon goes up before sunrise. Pack layers. Your ticks are saved in this browser only.
